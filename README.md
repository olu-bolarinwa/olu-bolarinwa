# Olu Bolarinwa

**Engineer. Builder. Thinker.**

I build software and explore a question that matters to me: **How can AI expand what people are capable of doing—not just help them do the same things faster?**

I've spent nearly two decades engineering and modernizing software systems, from legacy applications to cloud-native platforms. Building products taught me that technical sophistication matters less than whether people can accomplish something valuable with what we create.

## What I'm exploring

- **AI & human capability** — Practical tools that make useful expertise and sophisticated capabilities accessible to more people.
- **Technology & economic opportunity** — How context, infrastructure, and institutions shape who benefits from technology.
- **Public-interest software** — Small, open tools that help people navigate real problems, decisions, and systems.
- **Evidence over hype** — Reproducible experiments, clear limitations, and real outcomes instead of impressive demos alone.

## How I build

I work primarily with **Java, TypeScript/JavaScript, React, AWS, and cloud-native infrastructure**. I care about reliable systems, thoughtful product design, and using the smallest effective solution.

I'm working toward more public, open-source experiments. I'll share the code, the tradeoffs, and what the evidence actually shows as those projects become ready.
